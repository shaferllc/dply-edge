**Title:** dply: git-push hosting for Laravel with managed databases and autoscaling queue workers
**URL:** https://edge.dply.io/vs/forge
**Blurb:** dply runs Laravel apps in containers that scale out and sleep when idle, with managed Postgres, MySQL, Valkey and queue workers alongside, and the scheduler and migrations built in. The linked page maps each part of a Forge setup to dply, lists the trade-offs (no SSH, no persistent disk, cold starts), and says when Forge is still the better fit. Plans start at $5/month with that $5 included as usage.
