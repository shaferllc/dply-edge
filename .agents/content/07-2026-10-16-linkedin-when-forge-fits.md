A comparison page that only argues for your own product isn't worth much, so our dply vs Laravel Forge page has a section on when Forge wins.

Forge is still the better fit if you need SSH, a persistent disk, or software installed on the box itself, or if you want one fixed server bill however much traffic arrives.

If what you actually want is to stop owning the server, with queue workers that scale themselves and a database you don't run, that's what dply is for.
